Introduction

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute of Urban Environment, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), working under the supervision of Prof. Yong-Guan Zhu (Academician of CAS). My research focuses on environmental microbiology with a specialization in bacterial extracellular vesicles (EVs).

My research interests include:

  • 1) Environmental AMR Reservoirs: Identifying environmental EVs as pervasive antimicrobial resistance (AMR) reservoirs across habitats.
  • 2) Stressor Decoding: Elucidating human-associated & anthropogenic stressors driving EV-mediated ARG transmission.
  • 3) Biological Risk Assessment: Revealing EVs' impact on host immune health and disease outcomes.

Research Directions

Investigating how bacterial extracellular vesicles shape antimicrobial resistance and health risks across interconnected environments.

  • Environmental Reservoirs of Antimicrobial Resistance

    We investigate bacterial extracellular vesicles (EVs) as widespread vectors of antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs) across environmental interfaces.

    • Selective enrichment: EVs carry a greater abundance of high-risk ARGs than their source environments (Environ. Sci. Technol. 2022; J. Extracell. Vesicles 2022).
    • Plastisphere hotspots: Microplastics create interfaces that facilitate EV-mediated ARG transfer (One Earth 2026).

  • Human Stressors and Resistance Transmission

    We examine how human-derived pollutants and other anthropogenic pressures alter EV-mediated antimicrobial resistance (AMR) transmission.

    • Pollutant effects: Artificial sweetener mixtures increase ARG packaging into EVs and promote horizontal gene transfer (Environ. Sci. Ecotechnol. 2026).
    • One Health relevance: This work links human activity to the environmental spread of antimicrobial resistance.

  • Host Responses and Diagnostic Tools

    We combine mechanistic studies with single-cell analysis to define how environmental EVs affect host immunity and to improve their detection.

    • Immune reprogramming: Airborne EVs induce metabolic reprogramming in macrophages (Environ. Sci. Technol. 2023).
    • Raman-based diagnostics: Label-free Raman spectroscopy and deep learning enable rapid identification of EVs at single-cell resolution (Anal. Chem. 2022).
